Record clicks, inputs, and interactions from the Side Panel recording tab and build your automation live.

## Steps

1. Open the extension Side Panel and go to `Recording`.
2. Click `Start Recording` in the `Recording` tab.
3. Continue interacting with the page as usual (click, fill, select, etc.).
4. Each recorded step is saved immediately as it is performed. You do not need to wait for `Stop Recording` to persist steps.
5. Click `Stop Recording` when you are done to end the recording session.

<Callout type="info">Recording runs through the Side Panel workflow. Stopping recording ends capture, but step persistence happens continuously during recording.</Callout>

## Replay your recording

Return to the page you recorded on. Open **Automations** in the Side Panel and select the saved automation to run it. For an example with a visible result, follow the [Getting started walkthrough]([[docsref:/getting-started/introduction/]]).

See [Side Panel]([[docsref:/side-panel/overview]]) for opening methods, automation run behavior, and footer links.
